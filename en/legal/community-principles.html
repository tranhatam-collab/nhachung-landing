Community Principles

Nhà Chung is built on core principles to ensure a safe space for everyone.

1. Safety & Privacy

No filming, photographing, or recording others without explicit consent. Do not share others' personal information outside.

2. Respect

Everyone is equal. No discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or economic status.

3. No Manipulation

Do not use algorithms, psychology, or group pressure to coerce others' behavior. All decisions are voluntary.

4. Transparency

Important activities are logged. Everyone has the right to know how their data is used.

5. Accountability

Each person is responsible for their own actions. Violations may lead to account suspension.
