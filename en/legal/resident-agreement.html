Resident Agreement

This agreement applies to participants in co-living programs at Nhà Chung Đà Lạt.

1. General Commitment

  • Respect shared and private spaces.
  • Participate in communal cooking and cleaning as scheduled.
  • No prohibited substances, weapons, or dangerous items.

2. Safety & Privacy

No filming, photographing, or recording others without explicit consent. Do not share others' personal stories outside.

3. Conflict Resolution

If conflicts arise, notify the coordinator immediately. Do not let conflicts escalate and affect the community.

4. Termination

Nhà Chung may request departure for serious violations of safety or privacy principles. Refund per individual contract terms.

Note: Full resident agreement is signed before check-in. This page is a public summary only.
