Privacy Policy

A live-first privacy baseline focused on minimum data, transparent operations, and controlled internal access.

1. Data we collect

NhaChung limits collection to the minimum data needed to operate accounts, verify profiles, manage access, and support internal proof records.

2. How data is used

Data is used to run the platform, protect accounts, verify member state, support operating requests, and improve platform stability.

3. Protection and storage

NhaChung applies limited access, separated admin roles, and the principle of reducing unnecessary data exposure outside approved operating flows.

4. Data-related contact

Any request to review, adjust, or process data must go through the correct operating channel and the required verification steps before the system accepts it.
