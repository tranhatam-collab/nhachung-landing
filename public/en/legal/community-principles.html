Community Principles

A P0 baseline for keeping public surfaces, the app, and member stories grounded in real living, real work, and mutual respect.

1. Be real, do not overstate

Profiles, stories, contributions, and outcomes must be represented through verifiable facts. Do not publish misleading content, inflated claims, or promises of certain outcomes.

2. Respect privacy

Do not publish another person's name, image, quote, personal context, or data without clear consent and an appropriate usage scope.

3. Collaborate responsibly

Members keep operating commitments, respond through the correct channels, flag risk early, and do not use the system to pressure, harass, deceive, or harm the community.

4. Keep information in the right layer

Public content is for introduction and orientation. Member, project, contract, payment, and internal materials only open inside the app or an approved channel.

Public legal entities and scope limit
