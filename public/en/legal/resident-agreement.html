Resident Agreement

This P0 version is a principles framework. Rights, fees, duration, responsibilities, and case-specific terms only apply through a signed agreement for each case.

1. Participation requirements

Residents or members using a real space must complete their profile, identity verification, operating rules acceptance, and approval before receiving access rights.

2. Responsibilities in shared space

Participants protect safety, cleanliness, shared property, use schedules, and respect for other residents. Violations may result in limited or ended access.

3. Payment and separate agreements

Fees, deposits, returns, support, or financial contributions must go through a separate agreement, an approved payment channel, and appropriate receipts.

4. Exit and dispute handling

When participation ends, parties reconcile property, remaining obligations, and related data under the separate agreement. Disputes follow the operating process and applicable law.

Public legal entities and scope limit

International operating entity: VIET CAN NEW CORP (United States). Vietnam operating entity: BO CAU TRANG CO., LTD. Payment infrastructure: pay.iai.one.

Nhà Chung is not a real-estate marketplace and not a public capital-solicitation platform. No promised financial outcomes, no promised income. Any financial contribution requires a separate legal contract and independent legal counsel.
