FAQ

How is NhaChung different from a normal online community?
NhaChung does not optimize for attention. It optimizes for real action, real collaboration, real value, and a pathway that can move from online into real life.
Can people join for free?
Yes. People can start with a free account to understand the system, create a profile, and see the journey. Deeper action layers require the right plan later.
Does NhaChung guarantee income?
No. The system is built to improve the chance of earning through skill, projects, and real collaboration. Results still depend on ability, time, contribution, and market conditions.
How is the expert layer unlocked?
The expert layer is not sold directly. Members move into it through points, review, verified profile status, and real contribution.
